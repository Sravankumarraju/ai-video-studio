# Bhagavad Gita: research and Divine Wisdom series plan

Researched October 1, 2026. This is a complete chapter map and production plan, with a fully developed first verse episode. It is not a completed translation or individual scholarly review of every verse.

The Gita is a Sanskrit dialogue in the Mahabharata's Bhishma Parva. Arjuna faces a conflict of duty before the Kurukshetra battle; Krishna guides him through action, knowledge, spiritual discipline and devotion. It has 18 chapters and commonly 700 verses; some editions enumerate 701. Treat its teachings as spiritual and ethical guidance, and identify theological claims as teachings of the text. [IIT Kanpur introduction](https://www.gitasupersite.iitk.ac.in/srimad/intro)

## What the teachings cover

Chapter 2 presents the distinction between the changing body and enduring self, duty, nonattachment and a steady mind. This can support episodes about handling success and disappointment, while preserving the original spiritual setting. [Chapter 2](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/2/)

Chapter 3 develops karma yoga: perform responsibilities without making personal reward the sole motive; wise conduct also provides an example for others. Modern study and workplace stories should be labelled as our applications, not stories from the scripture. [Chapter 3](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/3/)

Chapter 6 concerns disciplined practice, meditation and the wandering mind. Use gentle, practical examples about returning attention; avoid presenting spirituality as a guaranteed cure for a medical condition. [Chapter 6](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/6/)

Chapter 11 describes Krishna's cosmic form. Cosmic artwork fits this topic particularly well, but the generated imagery is an artistic interpretation. Show contextual Krishna–Arjuna imagery for the dialogue itself. [Chapter 11](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/11/)

Chapter 12 connects devotion with conduct, including compassion and freedom from hatred. Episodes should connect devotion with how people treat others. [Chapter 12](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/12/)

Chapter 13 distinguishes the field and its knower. Chapter 14 discusses sattva, rajas and tamas. Explain these as philosophical categories; avoid assigning simplistic permanent labels to viewers. [Chapter 13](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/13/), [Chapter 14](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/14/)

Chapter 18 brings together action, knowledge, devotion, renunciation and surrender. Preserve the relation between practical duty and spiritual liberation, rather than reducing the whole book to motivational slogans. [Chapter 18](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/18/)

Different commentarial traditions interpret important passages differently. IIT Kanpur provides multiple commentaries that can be compared. Our Telugu explanations are original paraphrases; choose and identify a commentary when a doctrinal interpretation matters. Do not copy an entire modern translation into the series. [Gita Supersite introduction](https://www.gitasupersite.iitk.ac.in/srimad/intro)

## Ordered Divine Wisdom series

The selected production plan starts at **1.1**, then **1.2**, and continues in chapter/verse order, one video at a time. The channel names are exactly **Divine Wisdom Telugu**, **Divine Wisdom Hindi** and **Divine Wisdom English**, without an added tagline. Each language edition has equivalent content and shared images, with its own voice and captions. The channels can later cover other sacred books.

Each episode targets **3–4 minutes** and contains eight sections: welcome, learning overview, book context, verse and simple meaning, detailed explanation, modern example, conclusion, and a teaser for the next verse. Time the scenes from the actual recordings rather than accelerating speech. Retain a separate voice ID and shaped caption font for each language.

Episode 001 explains **BG 1.1**: Dhritarashtra's question to Sanjaya. It distinguishes the verse itself from commentarial interpretation and our modern family example. Episode 002 will explain **BG 1.2**. [Verse 1.1](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/1/verse/1/), [Verse 1.2](https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/1/verse/2/)

The local production playlist is saved in `data/productions/divine-wisdom/playlists.json`, with a browsable `playlist.html` and separate M3U8 files. Finished language versions appear in Story Studio's variant selector. This is a local production queue; a YouTube playlist has not been published. Remaining references are planned and require individual research, script review and narration. No printed page number is asserted without a specified edition.

## Optional later topic series

These are production recommendations, not claims made by the scripture:

1. Start with an accessible topic-based season of 12–20 videos. Lead with a recognisable question, then show the verse that addresses it.
2. Produce 18 chapter overview videos of about 5–8 minutes each. Explain the chapter's place in the dialogue and key ideas.
3. Build the complete verse series chapter by chapter, normally one verse per 2–3 minute video. Combine neighbouring verses when their meaning depends on each other; never split a passage just to increase the episode count.
4. Arrange the finished videos in both chapter order and topic playlists. Separate an episode's release number from its scripture reference: Episode 001 may explain BG 2.47; it is not verse 1.1.

Suggested first-season topics: duty and outcomes (2.47), equanimity (2.48), temporary pleasure and pain (2.14), desire and anger (2.62–63), responsible action (3.19), example-setting (3.21), self-discipline (6.5–6), returning a wandering mind (6.26), compassionate devotion (12.13–14), the cosmic form (chapter 11), the gunas (chapter 14), and synthesis (chapter 18). Only the current 2.47 episode has a finished production script; research and review each remaining passage before narration.

## Repeatable two-minute format

| Approximate time | Content | On-screen label |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 seconds | One concrete life question | Today's question |
| 10–30 | Original verse, read clearly | శ్లోకం + chapter/verse |
| 30–55 | Simple Telugu meaning | సులభమైన అర్థం |
| 55–80 | Context and a common misunderstanding | వివరణ |
| 80–105 | One relevant everyday example | ఉదాహరణ |
| 105–120 | A small action for today | ఈరోజు ఆచరణ |

Measure real narration before setting scene timings. Longer verses or explanations may need a longer episode. Do not unnaturally speed up the voice to meet the target.

For every episode, retain: edition, chapter, verse range, source URL, exact original text, Telugu-script transliteration, pronunciation notes, original Telugu paraphrase, context, example, daily practice, image prompt, artwork version, voice/model, audio processing, caption timing, review status and exported video version.

Use chapter–verse references as the stable identifier. A page number is only meaningful with a named printed edition and printing; do not invent one. Sanskrit written in Telugu script is a transliteration, not a Telugu translation. Keep those labels separate in the editor and video.

## Earlier standalone sample: BG 2.47

Title: **కర్తవ్యం చేయి — ఫలితానికి బానిస కావద్దు**. The episode distinguishes doing one's duty, reducing attachment to its fruits, avoiding pride in being the sole cause of results, and avoiding inaction. Student and employee examples are original modern applications. Audio finishing raises pitch by 0.5 semitone with tempo unchanged, adds 3 dB bass around 120 Hz, and uses narration volume 1.25 in the editor. Original recordings remain saved. In Story Studio: choose the new variant in **Prompts**, review each scene's artwork and saved prompt, then use **Voice & captions**, **Timeline** and **Preview & exports**. ## Review before releasing each episode

- Compare the quoted verse against the selected edition and adjacent verses.
- Review Sanskrit pronunciation by listening, especially when using a Telugu voice for Sanskrit. Automatic generation and timing checks do not establish correct recitation.
- Check the Telugu meaning separately from explanation and examples.
- Inspect portrait framing, shaped Telugu captions, readable line lengths and actual voice volume.
- Retain an approved export and its script, sources, prompts and recordings. Publishing is a separate user action.
